02 / YOUR FINANCING PATH

Choose lease/PPA or ownership.

Review your preferred path. Every lease/PPA request is configured with a 0% annual payment escalator preference; final offers depend on eligibility and written terms.

Option A

Lease / PPA

Focus on predictable energy costs without buying the equipment upfront.

  • ✓ $0-down options for qualified homeowners
  • ✓ Utility-bill replacement with a contracted solar payment
  • ✓ Often includes monitoring and maintenance provisions
  • ✓ Request a 0% annual payment escalator — no scheduled yearly PPA rate increase
  • ✓ Lower need for upfront equipment capital
Option B

Purchase / Own

Own your solar investment with a cash purchase or qualified loan.

  • ✓ Own the system outright or pay it off over time
  • ✓ Potentially greater long-term bill savings
  • ✓ Choose cash or financing, when available
  • ✓ Equipment may add long-term home value
  • ✓ Eligible ownership incentives depend on current rules

A 0% annual escalator means the contracted solar payment does not automatically rise each year; it does not guarantee your entire utility bill is fixed. Financing, contract term, annual rate, equipment availability and savings are confirmed in official provider documents.
