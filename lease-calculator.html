How accurate is your estimate?

We estimate that each 1 kW of solar produces approximately 1,000 kWh per year. Your estimated monthly payment is calculated using that production and a $0.20/kWh lease rate.

Your final monthly payment is verified after expected solar production is calculated for your specific roof. Panel arrangement, roof direction and shading may affect production.

This provides a useful starting estimate, although the final payment may be higher or lower, particularly if batteries or home improvements are included.

What Happens Next?

1. Receive Your Documents

Your request is reviewed and matched with your selected system. SunnyMac and Palmetto LightReach contract documents are then prepared and sent to you for review and signing.

2. Financing Approval

Final LightReach lease approval may involve a soft credit check and additional eligibility verification.

3. Sign & Confirm

Once the contracts are signed, you'll receive confirmation of your system and upcoming steps. A welcome call or phone verification may also be required.

4. Schedule Your Site Survey

Shortly after signing, expect a text message with instructions to arrange your site survey appointment.

You're in control. Your contract request is the next step toward going solar. You can review the actual documents before deciding whether to sign.
