BEFORE YOU BUY

What's not included in the box?

These are the main pieces of a storage system, not a turnkey installation. Your licensed electrician or qualified solar-storage contractor should finalize the site-specific parts list before an equipment order.

🔌

Battery cables & connectors

Correct DC cable size, length, lugs and terminals. The MidNite listing specifically excludes battery cables and recommends 4/0 AWG subject to installation design.

🗄️

Battery rack / mounting

The MidNite battery modules require a compatible rack or enclosure. Confirm approved mounting and any required busbars before ordering.

🛡️

Electrical protection

AC/DC breakers, fuses, disconnects, grounding, conduit and other code-required materials as designed by the installer.

🏡

Backup circuits

Whole-home versus essential-load backup may change the panel, transfer, isolation and circuit work needed.

☀️

Existing solar integration

If you already have solar, the installer must evaluate the present inverter, wiring, ownership and whether AC-coupling or inverter replacement is appropriate.

👷

Professional installation

Labor, permits, inspections and utility coordination are not included in the online package price.

Important: A matched battery-and-inverter package is not proof of a permitted installation. The installer and local authority must verify the exact applicable safety listings (including energy storage system approval), location requirements, and utility interconnection. Do not order based only on the product listing.

Common questions

Can an electrician install these?

A properly licensed electrician experienced with stationary battery storage can complete the electrical work where authorized. Local rules may also require solar-storage certifications, permits and inspections.

Will they work with my existing solar panels?

Potentially. The existing inverter and ownership agreement must be reviewed. Systems under a third-party lease or PPA may require provider permission before modification.

Do these kits include solar panels?

No. They are battery-and-inverter packages. A new solar project also needs a compatible solar array and the required photovoltaic installation materials.

Can I buy the equipment first?

We recommend having a qualified installer approve the equipment list first. This helps avoid buying incompatible components or equipment that cannot be permitted locally.
