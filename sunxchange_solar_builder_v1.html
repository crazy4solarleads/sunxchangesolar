02 / YOUR FINANCING PATH

Two great ways to go solar.

Explore a lease/PPA-style plan or ownership. Specific availability, pricing and terms are subject to qualification and the official proposal.

Option A

Lease / PPA

Focus on predictable energy costs without buying the equipment upfront.

  • ✓ Potential $0 upfront offers for qualifying homeowners
  • ✓ Utility-bill replacement with a contracted solar payment
  • ✓ Often includes monitoring and maintenance provisions
  • ✓ Possible fixed-rate or escalator choices, where offered
  • ✓ Lower need for upfront equipment capital
Option B

Purchase / Own

Own your solar investment with a cash purchase or qualified loan.

  • ✓ Own the system outright or pay it off over time
  • ✓ Potentially greater long-term bill savings
  • ✓ Choose cash or financing, when available
  • ✓ Equipment may add long-term home value
  • ✓ Eligible ownership incentives depend on current rules

Terms, ownership, payment increases, energy credits, incentives, transfer options and service obligations differ by product and provider. No rate or savings is guaranteed here.
