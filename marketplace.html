2. Purchase or Lease? See Both Options.

You don't have to decide today. Every request automatically includes a customized purchase demo and a lease demo for your selected solar package.

☀ Benefits of Solar Ownership

  • You own the solar equipment.
  • Potential eligibility for ownership incentives.
  • Potential long-term energy savings.
  • Multiple financing options may be available.
  • Greater control over your equipment and its maintenance.

⚡ Benefits of Solar Leasing

  • May require little or no upfront payment.
  • Predictable payments under the agreed terms.
  • Service and maintenance may be included.
  • Avoid purchasing the solar equipment.
  • Explore solar without an ownership commitment.

All pricing, incentives, maintenance provisions, payment escalators and eligibility are subject to actual offers and contracts.
