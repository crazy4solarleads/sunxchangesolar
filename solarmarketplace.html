☀ Why homeowners purchase

  • Own the solar equipment, subject to any financing terms.
  • May qualify for available ownership incentives, if eligible.
  • Potential long-term financial value and bill savings.
  • Choose from available purchase or financing options.
  • Control future equipment decisions subject to warranties.

🌱 Why homeowners lease

  • Potential low or no upfront cost.
  • Known payment structure under your agreement.
  • Maintenance and service may be included.
  • Get solar energy without buying the equipment.
  • Review lease terms before making a decision.

Incentives, payments, escalators, maintenance, ownership rights and eligibility vary. Your proposals and contracts determine the actual terms.
